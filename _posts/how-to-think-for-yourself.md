At a party I ended up talking to someone who was a therapist. The topic was AI
use and the ads on the subway for a 24/7 therapist agent called Dawn. She said
yeah, 99% of life happens outside therapy sessions, so you have to learn how to
deal with it.

I found interseting how she said (I think) you probably shouldn't use AI to go
through personal issues because it'll just reinforce what you said. Unless you
say something drastically wrong, I'd generally agree that yes, these agents more
or less reinforce what you prompted it with.

That got me thinking- these models have been trained to be helpful personal
assistants. When should we not use them? More broadly, it seems that AI is
becoming more and more powerful. How do you think for yourself?

- When to use AI and when not to? More broadly, how to think for yourself.
