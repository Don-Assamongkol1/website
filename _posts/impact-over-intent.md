Guosheng as an Example

Of how to not do an exit gracefully

Don’t want Bloomberg feeling that oh I’m only staying because of the visa. If
you have to go because they won’t push your start date, then you have to go.

His intentions were good. Probabilistically he was going to leave. But if he had
also left with 2 day notice, that’s ok too. Coworkers would’ve understood.

Better than leaving and then begging to come back again.

Similar to me giving my dad $20 bill of money. Perception matters.

The reality is, if my intent were really good, I probably would've put in more
effort and wrapped it in a nice post-card.

Lesson: perception matters.

Lesson: Similar to insurance; there are certain paths you don't want to end up
in, so you'll pay to not be in that path. For him, it's say you're leaving then
ask to not leave. Given that this outcome is like your house burning down i.e.
something you can't accept because it will be perceived terribly, you buy
insurance buy not entering that path.

It means your exit will likely be less ideal than giving a 2 weeks. But (a)
people will understand and (b) you don't ever want to end up in the worst case
scenario, so this less ideal exist is like the cost of avoiding the worst case.
